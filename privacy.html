Privacy Policy

Animal Sound Safari (the "app") by Pixydust
Effective date: 2026-10-22

Animal Sound Safari is a game for young children (ages 2 to 4) and their families. We take children's privacy seriously. This page explains in plain language what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • The app itself does not collect your name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.
  • Game progress (level, stars, animals collected) is saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. They are set up for children: non-personalized, rated G, and without the advertising ID.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (current level, stars, which animals you have collected, and the sound on/off setting) in the app's private storage on your device (localStorage). We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you reset progress inside the app (a grown-up check is needed) or uninstall the app.

Sounds and speech

All animal sounds are stored inside the app and work offline. The spoken voice uses the text-to-speech voice already installed on your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone or camera.

Ads

The app contains ads, shown by Google AdMob. A banner may appear at the bottom of the "Adventure complete" screen, and a full-screen ad may appear when a child moves on from that screen. Ads never appear during questions, at app start, or in the My Zoo, rules or fun-facts screens.

Because this app is directed to children, we tell AdMob to treat all use as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent, to serve only non-personalized ads, and to limit ad content to the "G" (general audiences) rating. The app does not request or use the Android advertising ID.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may process certain technical data from your device, such as your IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in line with Google's policies. We do not control that processing. Please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How AdMob uses data (Google AdMob help)

The app needs an internet connection only for ads. Without internet, the game works fully and simply shows no ads.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no social features, no chat, no user-generated content.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools (other than the Google AdMob ad service described above).
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No links that take a child out of the app to other websites without a grown-up check.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

The app is designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under the age of digital consent in the EU/EEA (GDPR). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us (below) and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

All data the app stores lives on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app, or use "Reset progress" on the start screen (a grown-up check is needed). Because we do not hold your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. For data processed by Google for ads, please use Google's own privacy tools (see links above).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
