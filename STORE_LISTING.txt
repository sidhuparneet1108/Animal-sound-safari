2. FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
==================================================
Can your little one tell a cow from a cat just by listening?

Animal Sound Safari is a gentle listening game for toddlers and preschoolers. Robo the friendly robot plays an animal sound, and your child taps the animal that made it. Every right answer earns a star, a cheerful chime and a happy cheer from Robo!

HOW IT WORKS
- Listen to the animal sound and look at the animal cards.
- Tap the animal that makes the sound.
- Collect stars and complete adventures. Each new adventure gets a little trickier.
- Meet new animal friends and fill up your own Zoo.

WHAT CHILDREN WILL ENJOY
- 50 animals to discover, from farm friends to jungle and ocean animals
- Animal sounds based on recordings (credits are in the app)
- Fun facts for each animal in the My Zoo book, which Robo can read out loud
- Big, colorful buttons made for small fingers
- Friendly spoken feedback - no reading needed
- Short adventures that fit a toddler's attention span

MADE FOR LITTLE ONES
- No accounts and no sign-up
- Works offline - sounds are stored in the app
- Game progress is saved only on your device
- Reset progress is behind a simple grown-up check

ABOUT ADS
This app contains ads. Ads are set up for children (non-personalized, general-audience content only) and appear only on the "Adventure complete" screen and when moving on from it - never in the middle of a question. A grown-up may like to sit with younger children. See our privacy policy for details.

Animal icons: Twemoji (CC-BY 4.0). Sound credits are listed in the app under "Sound credits".

Download Animal Sound Safari and start the adventure!

5. HONEST WARNINGS BEFORE YOU PUBLISH

A) Four animals use stand-in pictures because Twemoji has no emoji for them:
   - Hyena is shown as a plain dog
   - Walrus is shown as a mammoth
   - Seagull is shown as a generic bird
   - Cheetah is shown as a leopard
   A child (or parent) could notice. Consider custom artwork, or removing those four animals, before launch. Do not describe them as accurate in screenshots or text.

B) Eleven sound clips are NOT license-verified:
   fox, rhinoceros, zebra, kangaroo, koala, hyena, gorilla, panda, walrus, cheetah, raccoon.
   They came from YouTube channels and other places supplied by you (e.g. the ProSounds channel). I could NOT verify that you may use them in a commercial, ad-supported app. YouTube downloads are usually NOT free to reuse. Replace them with properly licensed recordings (CC0 / CC-BY / purchased) or remove those animals, before publishing.
   Also note: the cheetah clip is a PURR (not the typical cheetah chirp), and the gorilla and rhino clips are AI-GENERATED, not real recordings. The listing text above avoids saying 'real recordings' for this reason. Keep it that way unless you replace those clips.

C) Other sounds are credited in the app under "Sound credits" (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). CC BY-SA clips were edited; check that sharing the app under those terms is acceptable for you.

D) Ads: you must never tap real ads on your own device. The debug build shows Google TEST ads only.
